## Wait and diagnose

A missing Grant, blocked dependency, invalid member permission or unknown Runner binding must stop the affected operation. Offline eligibility differs from online-but-busy queueing. An Issue label alone is not completion evidence.

## Unknown writes and recovery

For partial Scope publication, preserve the journal and independently read back the Issue and native dependencies. Do not blindly replay writes. Audit is read-only; changes to protected work require applicable reauthorization.

Acceptance, CI and Merge failures require classification and fresh evidence after repair. Deployment failure does not permit premature Closure. This shell is not an operational recovery implementation; verified procedures remain Planned. See [Guides](../guides/).
